The Emerald Highlands

Signature Uluwatu Tour

The Complete Island Circuit

50 minutes

A sweeping circuit that gathers the whole island into one flight. Out over Nuanu and the sea temple of Tanah Lot, then down the coast past Berawa, Seminyak and Kuta to the seafood bay of Jimbaran. Dreamland gives way to the sacred heights of Uluwatu Temple, the secluded shore of Nyang Nyang, the arc of Pandawa and the chalk cliffs of Melasti, all watched over by the GWK. Banking inland across Serangan and Sanur, the flight crowns itself above Ubud before carrying you home.

Signature Penida Tour

The Wild Isles

55 minutes

Across the channel to Bali's rugged offshore realm. Skim the eastern shoreline by way of Padang Bai, Sampalan and Semaya, then Diamond Beach and its cove framed by pale cliffs. From there Nusa Penida reveals itself in full — the shallows where manta rays drift, the dinosaur-ridge of Kelingking, the stone window at Broken Beach, the jade strait to Ceningan and the blowholes of Devil's Tears.

Ride & Fly

Fly In, Ride, Fly Out

The helipad sits at the edge of the track. Land, change, ride — jungle trail, river crossings, the mud zone and the cave — then lift off again when you're done.

Ten minutes in from Nuanu, the aircraft waiting on the ground while you ride, and out the same way. The day starts with the ride instead of the road.

The ride, the guides, the briefing and the safety equipment are operated by Kuber Bali Adventure, who set duration, inclusions and eligibility.

Tell us your riding slot and we'll build the flight around it. Combined rates are quoted case by case.
